## Authority levels

- `reference-backed`: identity/structure/property source is traceable.
- `reference-derived`: normalized or transformed from a traceable source.
- `model-derived`: generated by a named deterministic model and parameter set.
- `exploratory`: useful for testing or discovery but not validated as real-world truth.
- `rejected`: failed structural, chemical, conservation, or provenance gates.

## Day 90 exit gate

- At least one reference-backed target and one model-derived/exploratory target complete the six-artifact suite.
- Required reports parse and cross-reference the same run/record identity.
- Mass and energy balances identify residuals and tolerances.
- Events preserve before/after state and provenance.
- Manifest hashes allow deterministic replay or explain why replay is unavailable.
- Every completed letter has an authoritative approximately two-page TeX.
